Privacy Policy

Vote Compass Israel is a non-commercial civic tool. We collect minimal data and do not sell or share your information with anyone.

What we collect

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use this site. Google Analytics automatically collects:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Approximate location (country and city level, derived from IP address)
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • How you arrived at the site (e.g. from a search engine or a link)

We do not collect your name, email address, or any information that directly identifies you. We do not have user accounts.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to distinguish between visits and track sessions. These cookies do not contain personal information and are used solely for analytics purposes.

The cookies set by Google Analytics typically expire after 2 years, or at the end of your browser session.

How your data is used

The analytics data we collect is used only to understand how many people visit the site, where they come from, and which pages they visit — so we can improve the tool.

We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

Google's role

Google Analytics is operated by Google LLC. Google processes analytics data on our behalf according to their own privacy policy. You can read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data collected through Google Analytics is retained for 14 months, after which it is automatically deleted.

How to opt out

You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting data about your visit by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

You can also block analytics cookies through your browser's privacy settings or by using a browser extension that blocks tracking scripts.

Third-party services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Both services may process request metadata (such as IP addresses) as part of normal network operations.
