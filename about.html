Vote Compass Israel

A side-by-side political platform comparison tool for the 2026 Israeli election.

What it does

  • Compare any number of Israeli political parties side by side
  • Filter by topics you care about using the checkboxes at the bottom of each column
  • Each platform position is tagged with multiple relevant topics
  • Unchecked topics show a coloured tally of how many hidden positions each party has — so you never miss something relevant
  • Click any source button to go directly to the original party platform page

Disclaimer

This tool is a research aid for voters — not an endorsement of any party or position. Platform positions are simplified summaries of official platforms and public statements.

Always consult each party's official platform before making decisions. Data may be incomplete or out of date.

The publisher makes no claims about how up to date data is, and is presenting this as best effort, in good faith. The publisher also did their best to keep biases out of the summaries, and invites the reader to raise any issues.

AI Disclosure

Generative AI was used to build the code of the website, and to translate party platforms to English. AI was not used in reading or writing platform summaries - that was all done manually, by human by hand.
